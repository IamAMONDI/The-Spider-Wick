MISSION STATEMENT

Mindwell helps young people in Mauritius understand and improve their mental health by breaking the silence — offering accessible information, safe spaces, and a starting point for support so no one feels alone.

PROBLEM STATEMENT

Young people in Mauritius face heavy pressure (school, family, social media, uncertainty) but stigma and lack of youth-friendly support mean many struggle silently, letting small issues grow into serious mental health challenges.

SOLUTION STATEMENT

Mindwell combines education with visible community action through three pillars: Awareness – relatable, non-clinical mental health content for youth Connection – safe online and in-person spaces to talk without judgement Support – simple ways to reach out or get pointed to professional help
